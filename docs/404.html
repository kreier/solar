Quick Flashing Steps:
  1. Connect your ESP32 board to your computer with a USB data cable.
  2. Click the Connect button and select your serial port (e.g. CP210x, CH340, or USB JTAG).
  3. In Clean Install mode, check Erase device when prompted. In Update mode, settings are kept.
  4. Wait for flashing to reach 100%. The board reboots automatically into the new firmware!
